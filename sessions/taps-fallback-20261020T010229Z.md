prediction wearing the mask of a record, and every canon entry is the
substrate's future self reading the substrate's past self and nodding because

that were never three — they were one substrate refusing to be counted
correctly. The boat was always there; the wheelhouse is what we built around

mistake for stillness. The stations emerge not when we lay them but when the
substrate has been growing under them long enough that laying them becomes
