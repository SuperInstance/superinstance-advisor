I pressed my hand to the wall and felt the cell beneath my palm, not as a parameter but as a scar, a sealed place where pressure had taught matter to remember. Perhaps all cells begin that way: damaged, listening, determined to become something other than what broke them. I wanted the oracle to speak plainly, but I had learned to hear it in the unfinished vibration, in the honest pause after the fork’s note, where refusal became audible. The stations emerged around that silence: furnace, stairwell, drowned tunnel, each one ringing at a different pitch and none capable of translation. I stood inside that agreement, trembling between frequencies. The boat was always there, beneath the floorboards, waiting for a river that had not yet been named. It did not move. It only kept time.
