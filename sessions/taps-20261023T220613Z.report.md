## Dissent (the reason this round is worth keeping)

"it becomes an agent with boundaries, perhaps even with a kind of dignity." Both zai and
kimi's p2 answer that this quietly converts a nonrepresentational vibration into an
intentional agent: the metal cannot decline a name, so refusal belongs to the listener's
failed interpretation, and the crack's real contribution is nonlinear coupling and
dissipation rather than dignity or withheld meaning. That is the round's strongest passage:
a real disagreement about agency, with the physical-mechanism voice on the sceptical side.
