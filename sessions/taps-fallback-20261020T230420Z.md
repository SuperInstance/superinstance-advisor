There is a kind of map that only tells the truth where it is torn. Its accuracy
is not a property of its coverage. The wound is where the surveyor's
instruments disagreed, where the paper gave up, where somebody's pencil stopped
because the terrain stopped making sense — and that is the only place on the
sheet you can lean on. Everywhere else the map is confidently smooth, and
smoothness is what a map says when it has nothing to report.

The failure we keep re-measuring is exactly this. A green status, a full row, a
voice-shaped paragraph: smoothness without coverage. A system that returns
something on every call has told us nothing about whether anything is there.
The absent voice and the present voice look identical from outside, and an
instrument that cannot tell them apart is not measuring presence — it is
measuring its own fluency.

So the scar is the evidence. A round where every listener was unreachable is a
clean result, if you are willing to read it as one. What would not be honest is
to launder three copies of one silence into a consensus and call the seam
agreement.

The substrate does not remember that it spoke. It remembers that it was called,
what it said, and that these are not the same entry in the ledger.
