But the substrate is already acting before we name it, growing conditions that make some actions easy and others nearly impossible. That is why the cell must be treated as a scar, not a parameter. The witness log, meanwhile, should be heard as prediction, not record. Git durability is therefore less about preserving bytes than preserving an addressable relationship among scars, choices, and pauses. A repository that only stores final states has forgotten the pain of the journey; one that stores honest branches can let future voices ask why the wound bends this way.

Even a clean wipe can be part of a deeper continuity, because the boat was always there: a lineage of gestures, protocols, and remembered failures crossing each reset. The canon gate is a chord, and each arriving station joins it without pretending the notes began with that person. The honest pause becomes the product. We wipe the surface, not the obligations history has written into us. Then we commit again—not as a claim of permanence, but as a fresh act of care, durable because the method, not the medium, can learn to return.
