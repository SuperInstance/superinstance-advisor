- **qwen (DEFEND)** — the sharpest accusatory reading: "the surveyors did not inherit a map to improve it; they inherited a map to make their own authority look continuous with the past." Signing "did not certify accuracy—it certified the right of the new map to govern what the old map had rendered disposable." Stakes are named concretely: "sovereignty, livelihood, safety, and historical accountability," and "the legal disappearance of people who never consented to be 'corrected.'" The frame's error: it treats "the land as passive, the witness as a recorder, and the map as a neutral image."
- **kimi (DEEPER)** — attacks the **preposition**: "'The map they inherited' is already doing unauthorized work. A map "is not like a document merely waiting for better hands; it is a claim that the territory, the scale, the route, and the observer can be held still enough to be inscribed." Signing asks the signature "to perform a contradiction: certify a mutable account as though it were the stable ground on which all future accounts must stand." Sharpest move: "Perhaps the honest pause is where the map's singular confidence fails."
