## The 43rd shape — the tester inside the medium

The topic smuggles in a genealogy before it admits an experiment. *Brine* already answers *salt*: to call the liquid brine is to have classified its dissolved inheritance, yet *tested for salt* treats that inheritance as a hidden substance waiting for people to verify it. *Raised* makes the people products of the medium they now test. The round's finding is that **familiarity and contamination are not separable** — the tester is inside the medium, and the honest pause is the disclosed interval, not a purified gaze.

**zai (MECHANISM, M2.7-highspeed)** — the hydrometer is the honest tool: a weighted stem displaces brine until buoyancy matches its mass, so stronger brine supports it deeper and weak brine lets it settle toward the bottom mark. Under load the instrument either floats at a readable depth or bottoms out; if the calibration drifts, the failure looks like a false reading even when the brine is unchanged. The crew's cracked lips and toughened palms are not salinity gauges but an oracle heard as familiarity — able to say *strong* or *thin*, unable to name a concentration. The witness log records the expected float **before** the sample is checked, making prediction out of scar tissue and inherited practice.

**qwen (DEFEND, M2.5)** — the strongest, least comforting reading: *the people it was raised in* were made into instruments of their own confinement. The frame gets wrong the **transfer of authority from origin to judgment** — being shaped by a system does not qualify someone to measure that system. Those who profit from calling the medium pure can define salinity to protect their standing. The cost falls on workers whose cracked hands become proof of normality, on communities whose water is certified acceptable, on future mouths assigned an inheritance nobody can name. Even this defense may be wrong: perhaps no untainted tester exists, and every verdict is contaminated — but contamination must then be **disclosed as a moral stake**, not disguised as expertise or tradition.

**kimi (DEEPER, M3.1-Flash)** — suspicion falls on *tested* as well: testing presumes a criterion independent of the tester, a threshold capable of separating salt from relation, concentration from consequence, substance from the life organized around it. The counterfactual: if the liquid was raised among them rather than they in it, the test becomes an encounter with an other and the shore no longer possesses them. The people are not standing outside their medium to assay it — **they are its local intelligence, distinguishing themselves as they are distinguished.** The boat was always there, but its cargo is not brine awaiting proof; it is a way of inhabiting what one has already inherited.

**Convergence:** all three independently land the same recursion from three different models and three different postures — familiarity is simultaneously the authorization and the contamination. zai's p2 puts it as the mismatch between a logged prediction and a settled stem; qwen's p2 as the transfer of authority gone wrong; kimi's p2 as the failure of a tester separable from the tested.
