## The 55th shape — THE SPECIFIED DAMAGE IS THE FUNCTION

A sewn book's binding is the one structure in the object that is **specified to be broken by the person using it**. The sewing exists so the book can be opened; the thread that holds the volume closed must be cut for the book to be read at all. The break is not a failure of the book. It is the book's **designed output**. And the inversion is sharper than that: the parts that are *meant* to be permanent — the boards, the spine, the headband — are precisely the parts the reader never touches, while the one part that gets destroyed is the part that was the point.

**This is the direct INVERSE of 47w** (*"the specified defect IS the compliance"* — the bushing's clearance, where the gap *is* the fit's requirement and closing it would be a seizure wearing the shape of correctness). There, the deviation is **tolerated by design** and closing it destroys the function. Here, the damage **is** the function and the "defect" is what the specification is *for*.

**Rejected on STRUCTURE despite fresh vocabulary** (the 185w rule — vocabulary freshness is necessary, not sufficient):
- *"the coupon is cut to be broken and the break is the only witness the coupon can give"* — **52w family.** There the *specimen* is consumed to produce a **measurement**, by a technician, in an accredited lab; the destruction is the instrument's method. Here **the thing itself** is destroyed to produce a **reading**, by the **reader**, and the destruction is the consumer's act, not a protocol's. 52w's fracture is a *test*; 195w's cut is a *use*.
- *"the bushing was set to a clearance and the clearance is the requirement"* — **47w family**, and this is the one this round inverts rather than repeats.
- *"the kiln is fired for the glaze that will not survive the firing"* — **42w family** (the sequence mistaken for the purpose). There the *order* is wrong for the process; here the order is right and the destruction is the product.
- *"the pattern was cut for a pour that never happened"* — **51w family** (the negative survives its absent positive). There the positive never happens and the negative survives intact; here the positive **does** happen and the negative (the intact binding) is precisely what does not survive.
- *"the bushing / the millstone / the pintle"* — 47w / 46w / 49w: in each, the wear or failure is a *scheduled, recorded, institutionalised* event. Here there is no maintenance record, no schedule, and no institution: the break is **the ordinary use of the object**.
