## 2. The 63rd shape

> **THE PROPERTY IS REAL, AND IT IS ONLY EVER OBSERVABLE IN A STATE THAT IS
> GONE BY THE TIME ANYONE LOOKS.**

A cart wheel is a wooden **felloe** — a shaped segment of steamed timber, curved
to the rim — with an iron **tyre** shrunk onto it. The fitter heats the tyre in a
charcoal fire until it glows and has grown past the wood, drops it over the
felloe, and drives it home with a tyre hammer and a set.

**The grip IS the interference.** The tyre has to be *bigger* than the wood to
hold on. The interference was bought with heat.

**Then the heat leaves.** The tyre cools onto the timber and grips by the
timber's own compression. The wheel is hung on its axle, dished, trued, and
judged — and every judgement is made on a wheel whose fit was produced by a
state that no longer exists. Nobody can *see* the interference. A ring gauge
says the wheel is round; a tyre hammer says the tyre is tight; a moisture meter
reads the wood. **None of them can re-run the fire.**

Three consequences, each of which is the shape:

1. **The fit is true, and the fit is not what is observed.** The tightness is a
   real property of the cold wheel. Nothing is manufactured, and it is not a
   comparison disguised as a property. It simply cannot be *looked at* in the
   state that produced it, because that state was a temperature and a moment,
   and both are gone.
2. **The judgement is made in a different state from the one it describes.**
   Every measurement of the wheel is a cold measurement of a hot fact. The
   record of the fit is a statement about the wheel made at a temperature the
   fact does not live at.
3. **The one-time is a property of the MAKING, not of the artifact.** A wheel
   can be re-tyred; a felloe can be replaced; the work is repeatable in
   principle. But *this* joint between *this* iron and *this* wood was set by
   *one* fire, and it cannot be reproduced on itself, ever. The record of it is
   a record of a state that can only be **left**, never re-entered.

### The canon mirror

A canon entry's coherence is a property of the substrate as it was **grown**,
not of the substrate as it is **read**. The entry is judged now, in the present,
by a reader holding a present-tense fact about a past state of the material — and
the making is over, and no gate can re-run it.

beneath its failure."*

**kimi** — MiniMax-M3 / DEEPER. *The tyre hammer lands on the anvil long before
anyone can say the wheel is fitted…* The premise attack: *"The claim that heat
ends before fit is looked at smuggles in a false sequence: heat as an episode,
fit as a visible property waiting to be discovered. Actually, the fire dying is
not the end of the heat; contraction belongs to it. Fit is made by several
agencies at once."*

**The three disagree about the topic's own claim.** zai makes the heat the
cause; qwen makes the heat a *liability* transferred to whoever is beneath the
wheel; kimi refuses the frame entirely and says the topic smuggles in a false
sequence, because contraction belongs to the heat. That is the 203w dissent axis
being opened by all three voices, and it is the sharpest three-way disagreement
in several rounds.

## 6. Canon-worthy

> *"The claim that heat ends before fit is looked at smuggles in a false
> sequence: heat as an episode, fit as a visible property waiting to be
> discovered. Actually, the fire dying is not the end of the heat; contraction
> belongs to it. Fit is made by several agencies at once."*
> — kimi, DEEPER, MiniMax-M3

**kimi's dissent is the canon-worthy paragraph of this round**, because it is
the only one of the three that refuses the topic's own causal story and gets
something *true* out of refusing it. zai and qwen both accept the sequence the
topic asserts (the heat makes the fit; the heat is over; the fit is judged
cold) and differ about who is harmed by it. kimi says the sequence is false —
that the heat does not end when the fire dies, because contraction is part of
the heat, and that a fit is therefore made by *several agencies at once*
(timber, iron, moisture, spoke tension, time) rather than by a single past
moment that has closed.

That is the 63rd shape turned inward, and it is sharper than the topic that
prompted it: the topic's claim is that a property is observable only in a state
that is gone, and kimi's answer is that **the state was never as separate as
the claim assumed** — the making was not a moment that closed, it was a
negotiation still in progress, and the reason the fit "cannot be re-entered"
is not that the fire went out but that it was never the only thing burning.
