predicts "pure" and later records "pure" **has not witnessed anything; it has converted expectation into
testimony.** Sharpest move: *"Until that exemption ends, the touchstone, not the bullion, belongs in the

on both sides: honest refiners lose their market, workers and communities lose standing, counterfeit metal
reaches the world.
