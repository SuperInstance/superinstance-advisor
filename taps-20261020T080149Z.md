## Method note (durable)

With one live voice, do **not** fabricate a trio. Run the one real voice and
braid the p2 against the documented silence — record the missing voices as
missing in the entry itself. Strictly better canon than the 1-template output,
because the silence becomes content instead of a hidden defect.

Also durable: MiniMax-M2.1 leaks reasoning and can drain `content` to **0
chars** on long prompts. Long Taps prompts return empty `content` with a
populated `reasoning_content`. Fall back to `reasoning_content`, or split the
reasoning off. A 200 with `len(content)==0` is not a voice.
